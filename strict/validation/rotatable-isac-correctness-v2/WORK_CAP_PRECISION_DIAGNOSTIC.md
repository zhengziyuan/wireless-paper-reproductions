# A precision repair is not yet a convergence remedy

The [actual case002 failed-step proof](actual-case002-armijo-step-highprecision-v2.json) separates an incorrectly rejected positive Armijo increment from satisfaction of the original gradient stopping criterion. A new [cold case001 first-block diagnostic](cold-case001-precision-repair-fixed10000-negative-diagnostic-v1.json) now tests that distinction explicitly.

The diagnostic keeps all 4 active antennas, 36 RIS elements, 2 users and 66 sensing directions; the original gradient, raw PR, documented non-ascent restart, literal retraction and Armijo rule are unchanged. Only the already-tested equivalent utility-difference arithmetic uses the more accurately computed original path sums. The unreported safety cap is predeclared as 10000 and the gradient threshold remains 1e-6. No state is resumed from the previous endpoint and no live bank or production source is changed.

The actual 10000-update run exited normally but did **not** converge: its final normalized gradient is 0.00665791668, above 1e-6. Ten predeclared selected trial checks at 50/80 digits all satisfy the unchanged increment-accuracy and original Armijo gates; this is not an arbitrary-precision certificate for every trial. The complete update history and previous 500/10000 capped results are retained, with their hashes bound in the public compact receipt.

Because changed acceptance rounding can change the binary64 trajectory, no bitwise original500-prefix claim is made. Nor is improvement, full six-scheme completion, full500-bank success, or a remedy proved. Further blind cap escalation is not proposed. The separate complete case002 WORK replay remains in progress and must be judged by its actual final state and original stopping gates, not by merely crossing the old failure point.

A fresh [read-only endpoint diagnosis](case001-stable10000-independent-gradient-tail-diagnosis-v1.json) independently checks all 36 phase-gradient components against the original scalar objective at 50/80 digits. The maximum analytic/finite-difference discrepancy is 1.23e-9; the independently estimated normalized gradient remains 0.00665791640. Local two-step finite-difference Hessians are indefinite and have an estimated absolute spectral condition of about 1.40e8. In the last 500 updates, the median accepted alpha is 6.1035e-5, median same-utility increment 1.17e-8, and 356 documented non-ascent raw-PR restarts occur. These are evidence of stiff, slow original updates rather than an inaccurate phase gradient or zero represented step. Finite-difference values are numerical estimates, not rigorous interval bounds; the observations establish neither a unique cause nor a converged remedy.
