# MIS sensing: scope of the reported binary recovery

For [Wireless Sensing with Movable Intelligent Surface](https://doi.org/10.1109/JSTSP.2026.3681476), the locally inspected accepted-author-version PDF (16 pages, publisher final version not checked here) describes a final rounding step on page 5 and binary thresholding on page 8. Those passages do not specify a numerical threshold, tie handling, or whether a row is selected by the largest relaxed assignment or by the largest saved SINR. They do not establish the authors' original implementation.

The independent reproduction's `strict/mis-sensing/engine.py` uses the largest relaxed assignment in each row. In saved start 4736 this selects zero-based column 21 for zero-based target 3, whose serialized approximate SINR is about 0.99414076398313345. The same saved fixed-phase SINR table has its row maximum at column 4, about 1.004938840356874. The saved relaxed weighted value is about 0.9941422146577252 and the saved eta about 0.9941422168240241. The saved argmax-X selection misses the saved eta by approximately 1.45284e-6, exceeding the recorded 1e-6 feasibility tolerance.

The existing [row-candidate diagnostic](FIXED_STATE_4736_ROW_CANDIDATES.md) shows that, at the stated tolerance and with independent rows, choosing each row's SINR maximum gives the unique rowwise candidate schedule; this is a diagnostic alternative, not a recovered author rule or a replacement for the retained failure. At zero tolerance the [fixed-phase bound](FIXED_STATE_4736_SIMPLEX_CEILING.md) remains about 1.02175e-8 below the saved eta for the serialized approximate SINR table. The alternate schedule cannot establish exact physical feasibility, the absence of other scheduling constraints, agreement with the original figure, or the result of another starting point. The physical forward model is not enclosed by a certified interval here.

The saved 6000-start assembly contains 5280 combined passes and 720 failures or unverified outcomes. It is not justified to attribute all 720 to this rounding example: the saved selected start 3072 instead reports an inner iteration-cap exit. These observations do not, by themselves, establish an error in the paper or warrant changing the existing reproduction, its tolerance, or budget.
